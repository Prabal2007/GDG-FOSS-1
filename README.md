# 🚀 GDG FOSSology ROUND 1

 *"Good technical writing is just as important as good code in the FOSS world."*

Welcome to the **1st Assignment of FOSSology ROUND 1**! 

In Open Source, writing brilliant code is only half the battle. The greatest projects rely on developers who can communicate clearly, document their work, and collaborate. This repository is your first proving ground to test your Git skills, technical depth, and ability to write compelling documentation.

---

## The Mission
1. **The Introduction (`PROFILE.md`):** We want to know who you are! You will fill out a basic bio-data template so that we can get to know you, your stack, and your goals.
2. **The Tech Deep Dive (`TECHNICAL_DOCUMENTATION.md`):** This is where you prove your mettle. You will write a captivating, technical blog-style document on your favorite technology. Show us how deep your knowledge goes!

## Repository Structure
Here is what you will find inside this repository:
* `README.md` 
* `CONTRIBUTING.md` - **READ THIS NEXT.** It contains the exact workflow you need to follow.
* `PROFILE.md` - Your personal bio template.
* `TECHNICAL_DOCUMENTATION.md` - The template for your technical writing challenge.

## 🏆 Evaluation Criteria
Your Pull Request (PR) will be graded on:
* **Git Workflow** 
* **Originality & Authenticity**
* **Technical Depth** 
* **Formatting** 
* **Clarity & Engagement** 

### STRICT ZERO-TOLERANCE AI POLICY
Because this assignment is explicitly designed to test **your** technical writing skills, all submissions must be entirely in your own words. 
* **AI-generated content will be flagged.**
* PRs containing AI-generated text will **not** be reviewed.
* Violators will be immediately **disqualified** and dropped from participating in all upcoming rounds.

*Show us your unique voice and your true understanding. Authenticity matters much more than perfection!*

Are you ready? Head over to **`CONTRIBUTING.md`** to begin your journey. Good luck!
